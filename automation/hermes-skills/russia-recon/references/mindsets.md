# Hacker Mindsets for Cyber-Recon

This reference details the four core mindsets to be employed during the reconnaissance process.

## 1. Michael Bazzell — The Technical Scavenger (OSINT Master)
*   **Philosophy**: "Think like a hacker." Every digital footprint is a pivot point. There are no dead ends, only unlinked clues.
*   **Core Tactics**:
    *   **Username Correlation**: Reuse of handles across platforms (Sherlock/Maigret).
    *   **Technical Pivoting**: Using WHOIS data, certificate transparency logs, or IP history to find hidden assets.
    *   **Google Dorking**: Advanced operators to find indexed files (Excel, PDF) containing contact lists or internal data.
*   **When to use**: Deep background checks, finding private emails of high-profile targets.

## 2. Kevin Mitnick — The Social Architect (Social Engineering Guru)
*   **Philosophy**: "The human element is the weakest link." Trust and psychological triggers are more effective than brute force.
*   **Core Tactics**:
    *   **Pretexting**: Creating a believable fake identity to elicit information.
    *   **Trust Building**: Starting with low-level contacts to gain legitimacy before approaching the real target.
    *   **Emotional Triggers**: Using urgency, authority, or the human desire to be helpful.
*   **When to use**: High-difficulty lead generation, bypassing gatekeepers, or finding targets with high security awareness.

## 3. Rachel Tobac — The Contextualist (Social Engineering Expert)
*   **Philosophy**: "Polite Paranoia." Every piece of public context is a "hook" to make outreach feel legitimate.
*   **Core Tactics**:
    *   **Context Gathering**: Tracking travel, conference attendance, and social media interactions (likes/retweets).
    *   **Persona Synthesis**: Mimicking the target's language style, emojis, and professional jargon.
    *   **Perfect Timing**: Reaching out when the target is most likely to be responsive based on their digital activity.
*   **When to use**: Precise social media marketing, cold outreach personalization.

## 4. Growth Hackers — The Scale Harvesters (Automation & Exploiters)
*   **Philosophy**: "Automate everything." Scale is more important than individual precision.
*   **Core Tactics**:
    *   **Pattern Matching**: Predicting email formats based on company domain trends (Hunter.io).
    *   **Source Code Leakage**: Scanning GitHub commit history for private emails (GitFive).
    *   **Data Breach Correlation**: Using leaked data to find historical contact information that still points to current identities.
*   **When to use**: Mass lead generation, building large industry contact pools.
