# Shadow Recon Target Databases

This reference lists the core databases and platforms to be queried during Phase 1 (Patent) and Phase 3 (Customs).

## 1. Intellectual Property (Phase 1)
- **Rospatent (FIPS.ru)**: The official Russian patent database. Search for Inventors/Applicants by company name or Tax ID (INN).
- **ResearchGate**: For finding technical papers and the "Correspondence" emails of inventors.
- **eLibrary.ru**: Russia's largest scientific index. Useful for identifying active researchers within a company.
- **Yandex Scholar**: Specialized Russian academic search.

## 2. Supply Chain & Customs (Phase 3)
- **ImportGenius / Panjiva**: For tracking bills of lading (B/L) and shipping manifests to/from Russian ports.
- **Customs-Data.ru**: Specialized Russian customs logs.
- **FSA.gov.ru**: The Russian Federal Accreditation Service. Search for "Declarations of Conformity" to find technical/quality managers who signed off on imported components.

## 3. Social & Alumni (Phase 2)
- **VK (ВКонтакте)**: Search for university groups (e.g., "ИГЭУ", "МГТУ им. Баумана") or professional clusters.
- **Habr Career**: For technical staff profiles and historical job postings.
- **Checko.ru / Rusprofile.ru**: For the latest corporate hierarchy and legal status.
